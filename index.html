Her story

Coco's road to the sunbeam was a long one.

  1. Chapter 1

    The Bronx

    Coco was found on the street in the Bronx, on her own and expecting kittens.

  2. Chapter 2

    An angel rescuer

    The stress of the rescue brought her labor on early. With her rescuer's help, she gave birth to four or five tiny kittens, all identical little orange copies of her.

  3. Chapter 3

    Brooklyn Cat Cafe

    Coco and the kittens moved into the kitten terrarium at the Brooklyn Cat Cafe, where they stayed until the kittens were old enough to be adopted.

  4. Chapter 4

    Her turn

    One by one, the kittens found homes. Then it was Coco's turn to be adopted.

  5. Chapter 5

    A FaceTime meet-cute

    We found her on the adoption site and met her over FaceTime. We loved her immediately.

  6. The best chapter

    Home

    We picked her up, and she's been ruling the apartment ever since.
